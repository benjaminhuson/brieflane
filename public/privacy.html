Privacy policy

Brieflane is a private tool, currently in a closed beta operated by Ben Huson (Huson DIY, UK). Contact: benhuson1@gmail.com.

What we access

With your permission, Brieflane signs you in with Google (your name and email address) and reads your Gmail using the read-only Gmail scope. It searches only for messages that look like brand collaborations or product review requests, plus later replies in those same threads. Other email is skipped: we store only its message ID, so it isn't fetched again, and never its sender, subject or content.

If you separately grant the optional Gmail send permission, Brieflane sends only the reply drafts you have explicitly approved and confirmed, one at a time.

What we store and why

For collaboration emails we store the message details and text, plus details extracted from them (brands, products, contacts, deliverables, dates, fees, shipping) with the quote they came from. This is used only to show you your deals, deadlines and suggested next actions. Google access tokens are encrypted at rest. Data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase (EU, London) and the app is served by Netlify.

AI processing

If AI features are enabled, the text of collaboration emails may be sent to Anthropic's API to extract details and draft replies. It is not used to train models, and every AI-extracted fact is checked against the original email text.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers above, who process it on our behalf. Brieflane's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

In Settings you can choose how long email text is kept (after which it is deleted), export all your data, disconnect Gmail (which revokes Brieflane's Google access), and permanently delete your account and all associated data. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.
